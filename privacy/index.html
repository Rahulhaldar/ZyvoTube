Privacy Policy

1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to ZyvoTube. This Privacy Policy governs the ZyvoTube Android application (package name com.zyvotube.rahul) and the official website hosted at https://www.zyvotube.in. It explains what information is stored on your device, what technical data is communicated when using network-connected features, and how you can exercise full control over your privacy.

ZyvoTube is designed from the ground up as a private, client-side media player and organizer. We do not operate proprietary user accounts, sell personal information, or perform cross-app user tracking. This policy describes our practices in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy standards.

2. No Account Registration

ZyvoTube does not require, offer, or maintain any user registration or sign-in mechanism:

  • The app has no ZyvoTube user accounts, profiles, passwords, or login screens.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media credentials to use the application.
  • We do not maintain any central cloud database linking your identity to your viewing habits, searches, or downloaded media.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a fluid and persistent media experience, ZyvoTube utilizes an isolated on-device SQLite database (Room) and encrypted-capable device SharedPreferences. All of the following information remains strictly on your local device:

  • Watch History: Locally logs video titles, identifiers, channel names, duration, and playback timestamps so you can resume where you left off.
  • Search History: Locally indexes search terms you enter to provide fast, recent search recommendations.
  • Local Playlists & Favorites: User-created playlists, bookmarked tracks, and liked videos stored in the local Room database.
  • Channel Subscriptions: Feeds and channels you follow are saved locally (channel IDs, titles, avatar URLs) without requiring a YouTube or Google account sign-in.
  • Download Missions & Files: Information on active and completed media downloads, including file paths, media formats, resolutions, and chunk offset metadata.
  • App Preferences: UI theme (Light, Dark, AMOLED black), default video playback quality for Wi-Fi and mobile data, SponsorBlock category toggles, Return YouTube Dislike settings, and audio converter preferences.

User Control & Deletion: You can clear your watch history, search history, playlists, and cached data at any time via Settings > Privacy & Data in the app. Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all private database records and internal cache files. Downloaded media files saved to shared public directories (e.g., Movies/ZyvoTube or Music/ZyvoTube) remain on your storage until manually deleted via your device's file manager.

5. Firebase Analytics

ZyvoTube integrates Google Firebase Analytics (operated by Google LLC) to gather aggregated, anonymized insights into application usage and feature adoption. Firebase Analytics collects:

  • Standard Lifecycle Events: APP_OPEN, session start, session duration, and app version numbers.
  • Engagement Metrics: video_play (video ID, truncated title, channel name), shorts_view, background_play, and feature_used.
  • Download Metrics: download_start, download_complete, and download_failed (recording format, resolution, duration, and error codes).
  • Search Metrics: SEARCH events recording query terms (truncated to 60 characters), filter type, and result counts.
  • User Properties: Global non-identifying flags including app_version, app_version_code, user_type (new vs. returning), and initial install timestamp.

Sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (such as passwords, private files, or precise GPS coordinates) is strictly excluded from telemetry. Data collected by Firebase Analytics is encrypted in transit via TLS and managed under Google Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

To deliver high reliability across thousands of Android device variants, ZyvoTube utilizes Google Firebase Crashlytics. When an unexpected crash or non-fatal exception occurs, Crashlytics automatically records:

  • Stack traces, class names, line numbers, and error root causes.
  • Device technical metadata: Manufacturer and model (e.g., Google Pixel 8), Android OS API level, free storage, and memory status.
  • Application state metadata: app_version, app_version_code, and non-sensitive operational breadcrumbs (e.g., stream retry attempts or audio muxing status).

Crashlytics reports contain no user-identifying data. Google retains Crashlytics data for up to 90 days, after which reports are permanently expunged.

7. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

ZyvoTube includes Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to provide timely service updates:

  • Upon installation, FCM generates a randomized app-instance registration token. This token enables Google servers to route notifications to your specific device instance.
  • ZyvoTube automatically subscribes devices to general topic feeds: all, announcements, and updates.
  • Notifications are categorized into distinct notification channels: General Announcements, App Updates (notifying you of new GitHub releases), and Maintenance Advisories (upstream YouTube extractor status).
  • FCM registration tokens are not linked to your personal name, email address, or browsing habits. You may silence or disable all notifications at any time via Android Settings.

9. Video Streaming and Search Operations

All video streaming and search queries occur via direct client-to-server connections between your device and media hosts:

  • ZyvoTube acts purely as a client interface. We do not proxy, intercept, or mirror video streams through our own servers.
  • When you search for content, your query string is transmitted to YouTube InnerTube endpoints to obtain search results, and to Google Suggestion APIs to provide autocomplete suggestions.
  • You can disable search suggestions, pause search history recording, or erase past search terms at any time in the app's settings.

10. Media Downloads and Background Playback

ZyvoTube offers media downloading and background audio playback with strict compliance to Android foreground service standards:

  • User-Initiated Data Transfer (UIDT): On Android 14+ (API 34+), downloads are executed using Android's official User-Initiated Data Transfer Jobs (RUN_USER_INITIATED_JOBS), providing high-priority system transfer notifications without legacy background sync restrictions.
  • Background Playback: Audio playback is maintained via Media3 MediaSessionService and declared under FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, enabling lock screen controls, notification actions, and screen-off listening.
  • No Cloud Media Storage: Downloaded files are saved strictly to your local device. ZyvoTube does not upload, copy, or redistribute your downloaded media files.

11. Microphone and Speech Recognition

ZyvoTube includes an optional voice search feature:

  • Voice search requires runtime approval of the android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO permission, requested dynamically only when you tap the microphone button.
  • The speech-to-text conversion is handled entirely by Android's native SpeechRecognizer platform service (such as Google Speech Services or your device's built-in speech engine).
  • ZyvoTube does not record, intercept, store, or transmit audio files to any ZyvoTube server. Only the resulting text query string returned by Android is received and inserted into the search bar.
  • You can decline or revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings without affecting manual text search or video playback.

12. Advertising Disclosures (Current Release Status)

ZyvoTube currently contains ZERO advertisements.

  • The current production release serves no banner ads, no interstitial ads, no rewarded ads, and no commercial tracking pixels.
  • The app contains no third-party mobile advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, IronSource, or Meta Audience Network).
  • Advertising ID Notice: The presence of permissions such as com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID in our merged Android manifest is introduced strictly as a default dependency artifact of the Google Firebase Analytics SDK. ZyvoTube does not access or use Advertising IDs for personalized advertising or profile building.
  • Should advertising ever be evaluated for future optional tiers, our Privacy Policy and Google Play Data Safety forms will be updated in advance of public release.

13. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or trade your personal data. Data is shared only under the following limited conditions:

  • Service Providers: Data is communicated to Google LLC (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, FCM, YouTube CDN) and GitHub Inc. strictly to fulfill app functionality, analytics, and software updates.
  • User-Initiated Features: Data (such as video IDs or track titles) is shared with community APIs (SponsorBlock, DeArrow, LRCLib) only when those features are activated.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation, or to protect the safety, security, and rights of our users.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Device Records: Retained locally on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete them. You can wipe search history, playback logs, or playlists at any time via Settings > Privacy & Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Removes all sandboxed app databases, cached media parts, and SharedPreferences. Files saved to external public folders can be erased using any Android file explorer.
  • Firebase Analytics Telemetry: Stored by Google for 2 to 14 months according to standard Google Analytics retention windows.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Retained by Google Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, after which it is deleted.
  • FCM Tokens: Expunged automatically upon app uninstallation or token regeneration.

15. Data Security Measures

We employ multi-layered industry standards to protect information handled by the application:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network requests made by ZyvoTube to Firebase, YouTube, Google, and community APIs strictly enforce HTTPS/TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Android Application Sandboxing: All local database records, preferences, and cached assets reside inside Android's protected application sandbox, inaccessible to other third-party applications on non-rooted devices.
  • No Sensitive Credentials: Because ZyvoTube uses no user passwords or credit card transactions, no sensitive financial or identity credentials exist on our systems.

16. Children's and Teens' Privacy

ZyvoTube is a general-audience media player application:

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in applicable European Union jurisdictions), in compliance with COPPA and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • Because the application provides access to third-party media content hosted on YouTube, parents and guardians are strongly advised to monitor their children's viewing habits and consult YouTube's content guidelines.
  • If you become aware that a child has provided personal information to third-party services through ZyvoTube without parental consent, please contact us immediately to take appropriate measures.

17. User Choices and Device Permissions

You have full autonomy over the permissions granted to ZyvoTube. All non-essential permissions can be disabled without losing access to basic offline and online playback:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Controls playback controls and announcement alerts. Manageable via Android Settings > Apps > ZyvoTube > Notifications.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Enables voice search. Requested on-demand; can be denied or revoked without impairing text search.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used only if importing external audio files or saving downloads to shared storage.
  • RUN_USER_INITIATED_JOBS: Enables reliable background downloading on Android 14+ without battery-draining wake locks.
  • In-App Privacy Toggles: You can toggle "Pause watch history" and "Pause search history" directly in Settings to browse without generating local records.

18. International Data Processing

Because ZyvoTube communicates directly with globally distributed services (including Google Firebase, Google Cloud, YouTube CDN, GitHub, and Cloudflare-fronted community APIs), technical data such as IP addresses and error reports may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States and the European Economic Area. By using these features, you acknowledge that international data transfers may take place subject to the privacy frameworks of the respective providers.
